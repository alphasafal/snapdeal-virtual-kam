# Recording script (aim for two minutes)

Timings below come from the live Hindi call of 10 November: Asha takes about two seconds to
start replying and speaks for 8 to 20 seconds per turn. A clean call with two actions is
about two minutes. Trim the ringing at the start and any silence at the end; do not cut
inside the conversation.

## Before you press record

1. Close other apps and browser tabs. The laptop runs the agent, and a busy laptop makes her slow.
2. Use the laptop speakers, not headphones. The built-in screen recorder (Cmd+Shift+5,
   Options, Microphone: MacBook microphone) records only the microphone, so Asha's voice must
   come out of the speakers to be heard in the video.
3. Record ten seconds as a test and play it back. You must hear both your voice and Asha's.
4. On the home page choose **Sharma Home Essentials**. Language: **Hinglish**. Voice: **Sarvam**.
5. Sit somewhere quiet. Background noise is heard as speech and interrupts her.

## The call

Say each line once, clearly, and wait for her to finish. English lines work the same way.

| # | You say | What you should see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Click **Start call** and wait. | She greets you by name. |
| 2 | "मेरी sales इस महीने बहुत गिर गई हैं। क्यों?" (*My sales dropped a lot this month. Why?*) | The drop, the main cause, and one question. |
| 3 | "Supplier ने delivery late कर दी थी।" (*My supplier delayed the shipment.*) | She recommends restocking the best sellers and asks: shall I create the plan? |
| 4 | "हाँ, बना दीजिए।" (*Yes, do it.*) | The restock plan appears under "Agreed on this call". She asks about pausing listings. |
| 5 | "हाँ, कर दीजिए।" (*Yes, go ahead.*) | The paused listings appear there too. She asks about a safety buffer. |
| 6 | "नहीं, वो रहने दीजिए। बस इतना काफ़ी है।" (*No, leave that one. That is enough.*) | Nothing is added. She books the follow-up and gives the summary. |
| 7 | "नहीं, बस। धन्यवाद।" (*No, that's all. Thank you.*) | She says goodbye and hangs up by herself. |
| 8 | Click **Team dashboard**, open the call. | Summary, actions, transcript, figure check. Stop recording. |

Step 6 is worth keeping: it shows she takes no for an answer and saves nothing.

## If you have 20 seconds to spare, add one of these

- **Interrupt her** mid-sentence in step 3 with "एक minute, ये restock plan क्या है?". She should
  stop and answer. This shows the conversation is two-way.
- **Ask instead of agreeing** in step 4: "इससे क्या फायदा होगा?". Nothing is saved until you
  actually say yes. This shows the consent check.

## For the live walkthrough (not the two-minute video)

- Click **Simulate a week passing**, then call again: she opens with what was agreed.
- Trendz Fashion Hub (rival pricing) and Gadget Galaxy (late dispatch) get different diagnoses.
- **Try your own numbers** with sales that went up: she should not invent a problem.
- Say "मेरा payment अटका हुआ है": she hands over to a person.
- After a save, say "वो हटा दीजिए": the action is removed.

## If something goes wrong while recording

| What happens | What to do |
|---|---|
| Ringing for more than 15 seconds | End the call and start again. The first call after a restart is the slowest. |
| A long silence after you speak | Wait five seconds. A stalled request is retried by itself. |
| She repeats a question you answered | Say the answer again in a full sentence, such as "हाँ, कर दीजिए". Then tell me, with the time of the call. |
| She says a wrong number | Finish the call. The figure check on the call page will flag it; use another take. |
